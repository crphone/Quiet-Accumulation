# Quiet Accumulation

Binance Agent OS Mini Hackathon, Track A submission (Trading Workflows)

Quiet Accumulation is an AI agent that scans spot markets for volume divergence, moments where trading volume spikes while price barely moves. That pattern often shows up when large buyers are quietly building a position before a bigger price move becomes visible on a normal price chart.

## Why this exists

Most simple trading agents just watch price against a moving average or RSI level. Quiet Accumulation looks underneath the price chart, at the relationship between volume and price, to surface a signal most beginner tools miss entirely. It never touches futures, margin, or leverage, spot data only.

## How it works

Built on the Binance MCP Server (https://agent.binance.com/mcp/agentic), connected with Market data scope only, no Account, no Trade, no Transfer, so no funds are ever at risk and no permissions beyond reading public market data are needed at all.

The full agent logic, the volume baseline calculation and the divergence rule, lives in AgentQuiet.md.

## Try it yourself

1. Open the Claude app (desktop or mobile).
2. Go to Settings, Connectors, Add custom connector, and paste in https://agent.binance.com/mcp/agentic
3. Grant Market data scope only.
4. Paste the contents of AgentQuiet.md in as your system prompt or project instructions.
5. Ask: "Run my Quiet Accumulation scan."

## Why this could scale

A lightweight scanner like this could sit inside the Binance app as a daily nudge, drawing users back to check spot markets they might otherwise ignore, using a signal type most retail tools do not surface. It needs only the lowest possible permission level, Market data, so it is safe to offer to any user by default with zero account risk.
